Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Tom Shubert IT Consulting ("I," "me" or "my") collects through tomshubert.com (the "site") and while providing IT services, and how it is used.

Information you give me

The site has no accounts or sign-ups. If you call, email, write to me or use the contact form, I receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, business, phone number, email address and a description of what you need. I use it only to respond to you and to provide the services you ask for.

Information collected automatically

The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and I don't run analytics that identify individual visitors. Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to my email through Google and are not stored on the website. Like any website, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes technical data such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. The site's fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser loads them. Those providers handle that data under their own privacy policies.

Client systems and data

Supporting your technology may require access to your computers, accounts, email and files. I access client systems and data only as needed to perform the work you request, keep what I learn confidential, and do not copy, keep or use your data for any other purpose except as required by law. Passwords and access you provide are used only for your work.

Sharing

I do not sell or rent personal information, and I don't share it for advertising. I share information with others only when needed to do the work you've asked for (for example, opening a support case with Microsoft or setting up service with a phone provider for you), when you ask me to, or when required by law.

Security and retention

I use reasonable measures to protect the information I hold and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above, for my business records, or as required by law. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, subject to any records I'm required to keep. Contact me using the details below.

Children

The site and my services are intended for businesses and adults, not for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
