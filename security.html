Security

Practical protection for small offices

Most break-ins start with a stolen password or a convincing email. I set up the practical protections that stop most of them, keep them working, and I'm there when something gets through.

Multifactor sign-in

A second step at sign-in for everyone, so a stolen password alone isn't enough to get into your email or files.

Email filtering

Filtering that catches phishing, spoofed senders and malware before they reach your inbox, plus the domain settings (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) that stop others from sending email as you.

Protected computers

Protection and updates on every computer, so known holes get patched and threats get stopped.

Optional 24/7 monitoring

Around-the-clock watch on your Microsoft 365 accounts for sign-ins from unexpected places, backed by a 24/7 monitoring center. If something looks wrong, I get the alert.

Backups

Computer, server and Microsoft 365 backups set up and kept running, so a dead drive or a ransomware attack doesn't take your files with it.

Spotting scams

Helping your team recognize phishing emails, fake invoices and phony login pages before they work. For free scam alerts anyone can use, see my site FAKES.COM.

People who leave

When someone leaves, their access is shut off promptly, their devices are secured, and their mailbox and files are kept for whoever takes over.

When something goes wrong

Locking down a hijacked account, cleaning up infected computers, getting you back up and running after an attack, and bringing in specialists when a situation calls for them.

Secure remote access

Encrypted VPN and remote desktop connections so people can reach the office from anywhere without leaving the door open.

A real example

Someone in Germany signed in to a law firm employee's Microsoft 365 account. The firm's monitoring service flagged the sign-in right away, and I locked the account down and shut the intruder out before any firm or client data was taken.

No one can make any system completely safe, but these steps stop the most common problems and limit the damage when something does get through.
